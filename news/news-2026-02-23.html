Ramadan Party 2026

February 23, 2026

The Hokkaido Islamic Society in collaboration with the Hokkaido University Islamic Cultural Club (HUICC) warmly invites you to join Ramadan Party 2026.

This special event offers an opportunity to learn about Ramadan and the significance of fasting in Islam, followed by a joyful iftar (breaking of the fast) featuring delicious international halal cuisine.

  • Date: Sunday, March 8, 2026
  • Time: 17:00–19:30
  • Venue: Sapporo City Higashi Kumin Centre
  • Admission: Free of charge

Program Schedule

  • 17:00 – Registration
  • 17:15 – Opening & Qur'an Recitation
  • 17:20 – Lecture
  • 17:47 – Iftar (Dinner)
  • 18:30 – Quiz & Gifts
  • 18:45 – Q&A
  • 19:00 – Exhibition & Networking

We welcome students, faculty members, families, and community members to experience the spiritual and cultural atmosphere of Ramadan together.
